## Data Summary & Variables

### 1. Reel Good Dataset
* **Source:** [Reel Good Data (Title+Service+Genre+Tag List).csv](file:///Users/milanmiric/Desktop/MBA%20PM%20-%20Intro%20Workshop/Data/raw%20data/Reel%20Good%20Data%20(Title+Service+Genre+Tag%20List).csv)
* **Scope:** Cross-sectional coverage of movie and TV show availability, ratings, and genre tags across 150+ streaming services.
* **Shape:** 287,834 rows, 19 columns.
* **Key Variables:**
  * `Title`: Content title.
  * `Type`: Format type (`tv` or `movies`).
  * `Service`: Platform hosting the content (e.g., `netflix`, `hbo_max`).
  * `Genre` & `Tag`: Descriptive labels and trope lists (e.g. `['Drug', 'Doctor']`).
  * `IMDB` (0.0–10.0 scale) & `ReelGood` (0.0–100.0 scale): Ratings.
  * `AgeRating` & `Rated`: Content age advisory levels.
  * `Released Year` & `Duration Year`: Premiere year and run span.
  * `Seasons`: Number of seasons (for TV shows).
  * `Rent or Buy Available` / `Exclusive Service`: Boolean markers.

### 2. Ampere Analytics Catalogues (2016–2020)
* **Source:** [Ampere Analytics Data - OPTIONAL/](file:///Users/milanmiric/Desktop/MBA%20PM%20-%20Intro%20Workshop/Data/raw%20data/Ampere%20Analytics%20Data%20-%20OPTIONAL)
* **Scope:** Monthly catalog tracking of available titles in the United States from 2016 through 2020 for three major platforms.
* **Shapes:**
  * Netflix: 410,683 rows
  * Hulu: 331,464 rows
  * Amazon Prime: 969,160 rows
* **Key Variables:**
  * `YearMonth`: Date format `YYYYMM` tracking the catalogue snapshot month.
  * `Platform`: Streaming platform (`Netflix`, `Hulu`, `Amazon`).
  * `Content Type`: Content format (`Movie` or `TVSeason`).
  * `Title`: Title of the content.
  * `First Release Year`: Premiere year.
  * `Primary Genre` / `SecondaryGenre` / `Genre`: Granular categorization.
  * `Duration`: Playtime duration in minutes.
  * `Duration Group`: Runtime bracket grouping (e.g., length range).
  * `Number of Episodes`: Count of episodes per season.
  * `Primary Production Country` & `Primary Production Company`: Origin details.
